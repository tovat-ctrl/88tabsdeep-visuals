Hybrid, Online & NYC-Area Law Schools

Every ABA-approved hybrid or online JD program found, plus every law school in New York State and within commuting range of NYC. Each row shows program length, the posted fall 2027 application deadline, and whether the school holds an ABA variance to accept JD-Next instead of the LSAT/GRE.

New York bar limit on online credits

New York caps distance-education credit at 15 and allows none in the first 28 credits. Graduates of most hybrid and online programs must petition the NY Court of Appeals for a waiver before sitting for the NY bar. Confirm each program's track record with these waivers.

What "JD-Next: Yes" means

The school holds an ABA variance to admit with a JD-Next score alone. Policies still vary; some schools treat it as a supplement and prefer an LSAT. "No" means no variance; you can still send JD-Next as an addendum alongside an LSAT or GRE.

Tuition and bar pass rates

Tuition is shown as each school publishes it: per year, per semester or per credit, with the academic year. "ABA 509" figures come from schools' required ABA disclosures and may be a year behind. Bar pass is the 2025 first-time rate for the whole school, residential and online students combined, compared with the average for the state where graduates tested.

Reading the deadlines

Most schools admit on a rolling basis, so priority dates matter for scholarships. A † marks a school-wide date where the online or hybrid track may run on its own calendar.
